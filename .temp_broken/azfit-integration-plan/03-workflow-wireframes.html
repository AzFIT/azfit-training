■ Strong — Workout Tracker Gym Log

What Makes Strong Unique

Strong is the gold standard for in-workout logging UX. It balances speed (log a set in 2 taps) with depth (tap any exercise for rich analytics). The dark mode with green accents is optimized for gym lighting. 4.8 rating, 150K downloads, $500K/mo revenue.

What AzFIT Should Take From Strong

  • Previous session data inline — always show last workout's weight/reps next to input fields
  • Auto rest timer — circular countdown between sets, auto-starts on completion
  • Visual plate calculator — when a trainer prescribes 80kg, show the client which plates to load
  • Checkmark to complete — satisfying single-tap to finish a set
  • Layered information — keep workout screen minimal, tap for detail (charts, PRs, history)
  • Superset grouping — visual indicator when exercises are paired

■ Freeletics — AI Conversational Training

What Makes Freeletics Unique

Freeletics pioneered conversational AI program generation. Instead of complex forms, the AI asks one question at a time: "How much time do you have?" "Any equipment?" "How much space?" Each answer adapts the next question and the final workout. 59 million users. The Coach+ feature adds ChatGPT-style conversational guidance.

What AzFIT Should Take From Freeletics

  • Conversational program builder — Chat-style wizard for program creation (one question at a time)
  • Training journey roadmap — Visual phased timeline showing client progress
  • Tappable pill buttons — No complex dropdowns, just tap to answer
  • Week-by-week session markers — Show Mon/Wed/Fri completion status
  • Milestone callouts — "Next: 1RM Test in 4 weeks" with improvement stats
  • Progressive disclosure — Don't overwhelm, reveal one step at a time

■ 1FIT — All-in-One Coaching Platform

What Makes 1FIT Unique

1FIT is the most sophisticated coach-client management platform. It features client roadmaps with fully customizable phases, daily check-ins, recovery tracking (HRV, sleep, readiness), lead generation tools, and group management. The "Provide Clarity, Incentivise Accountability, Enhance Adherence, Achieve Results" framework is its design philosophy.
